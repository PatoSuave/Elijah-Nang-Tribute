# Project Brief

## Working Title
**Elijah Nang Tribute**

## Domain
**nangsoul.com**

## Purpose
Build a respectful memorial and archive website honoring Elijah Nang. The site should help visitors:
- learn who he was
- discover his music and public profiles
- access his work across platforms
- experience a thoughtful and emotionally grounded presentation of his artistic legacy

## Tone
- respectful
- cinematic
- archival
- artistic
- emotionally restrained
- not commercial

## Core User Experience
The site should feel like a carefully preserved digital memorial rather than a startup landing page or fan spam page.

## Audience
- existing fans
- new listeners discovering his work
- people arriving via the reclaimed domain
- anyone seeking a clean central destination for Elijah Nang's public content

## Visual Direction
- dark theme
- elegant typography
- soft contrast
- editorial layout
- subtle texture or grain acceptable
- minimal but polished interactions

## Functional Scope for v1
- one-page website
- responsive layout
- artist intro / bio
- featured works section
- outbound links to music and social platforms
- memorial disclaimer in footer
- deploy-ready on Vercel

## Out of Scope for v1
- CMS
- user accounts
- tribute submissions
- database
- custom backend
- hosting copyrighted media directly

## Success Criteria
- fast load time
- clean mobile experience
- tasteful design
- clear outbound paths to Elijah Nang's public work
- strong memorial framing without legal ambiguity
