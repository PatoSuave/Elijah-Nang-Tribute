# Legal and Editorial Notes

## Positioning Recommendation
Unless you have explicit permission from family, collaborators, or the estate, present the site as an **unofficial memorial archive**.

## Avoid
- uploading copyrighted songs directly
- rehosting copyrighted videos
- using album artwork you do not have rights to unless platform embedding or fair linking context is clearly appropriate
- implying that the site is the official estate or official artist site

## Safer Approach
- write original memorial copy
- link to official/public platform pages
- embed from YouTube or Spotify where allowed
- use clearly labeled external links
- include an explicit memorial disclaimer in the footer

## Editorial Tone
The writing should feel:
- respectful
- restrained
- sincere
- archival
- non-sensational

## Messaging Principle
The site should preserve access and remembrance, not claim ownership over Elijah Nang’s work.

## Suggested Disclaimer
“This is an unofficial memorial archive created to honor Elijah Nang. All music, video, artwork, trademarks, and platform content belong to their respective owners.”
